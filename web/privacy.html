NoMoreX Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

NoMoreX is a strength-training log. You record personal bests, build workouts and programs, and log your training sessions. This policy explains what information the NoMoreX app collects, why, who it's shared with, how long it's kept, and how to have it deleted. It covers the iOS and Android apps and the web app.

NoMoreX is made and run by Michael Penrod, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your information go to therealnerdybeast@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Email address, used to sign in and to identify your account.
  • Password. Our authentication provider stores only a one-way hash of it; we never see it.
  • User ID, a random identifier created with your account.

Profile

  • Display name, if you set one. It's optional.
  • Preferences: your weight unit (kg, lbs or both) and the formula used to estimate your one-rep max.

Training data

  • Personal bests: the lift, weight, reps and date.
  • Workouts and programs you create or save, including their exercises, sets, notes and any custom exercises you add.
  • What you log during a session: which sets you completed, the weight you used and the effort (RPE) you record, and when the session started and finished.

Crash and diagnostic data

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to our crash-reporting provider (Sentry). A report includes what went wrong and where in the app, the app version, and your device model and operating system version. It's linked to your account by your user ID and email address so we can follow up on problems you run into.

Files you upload for AI import

Only if you use AI import: the photos or PDF you choose, and what was read from them. See AI import.

What we don't collect

NoMoreX has no advertising and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. We don't collect your contacts, your precise location, or health data from Apple Health or Google Health Connect. Photos you import have their location data removed on your device before they're uploaded.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: keep your account, store your training history, and work out weights from your personal bests.
  • To show what you choose to publish to other users (see below).
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To run AI import when you ask for it, and to enforce its usage limits.

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build an advertising profile.

3. What other people can see

Everything you create is private unless you make it public. When you make a workout or program public, other NoMoreX users can see it in Community, along with your display name (or a generic label if you haven't set one). Your email address is never shown to other users.

The people who run NoMoreX (app administrators) can see a list of accounts with each account's email address, display name, sign-up date and last sign-in date, so they can run the service and manage who has administrator access. They can't see your private workouts, programs or personal bests in the app.

Other users can save a copy of a public workout or program to their own account. A copy belongs to them: it stays in their account, with your display name as it was when they copied it, even if you later make the original private or delete it.

4. AI import

AI import reads a workout or program from photos or a PDF you choose. It's optional, and the app asks you to accept a notice before your first import.

  • Your files are uploaded to our storage and sent to Anthropic, whose AI model, Claude, reads them to find the workout or program. Anthropic processes them on our behalf under its commercial terms, which don't allow it to train its models on them. Anthropic deletes what we send it, and what its model returns, within 30 days. It can keep them longer only where the law requires, or for up to 2 years if its automated safety systems flag them as breaking its usage policy.
  • You review what was read before anything is saved to your account.
  • Your uploaded files are deleted from NoMoreX once you save or discard the import, or after 7 days if you do neither. What was read is deleted after 7 days if you haven't saved it.
  • We keep a small record of each import (when it ran, whether it succeeded, and how much processing it used) to enforce usage limits and manage costs. It doesn't include your files or what was read from them.
  • Please don't upload photos of other people or personal details such as names, addresses or medical notes.

5. Service providers

We use these companies to run NoMoreX. They process information only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. Your account and training data are stored here, in the United States (Northern Virginia).
  • Sentry: crash and error reports.
  • Anthropic: reads the files you upload for AI import, and only when you use it.
  • GitHub: hosts the web app and this page.
  • Apple and Google: distribute the apps through the App Store and Google Play under their own privacy policies.

If you use NoMoreX from outside the United States, your information is transferred to and stored in the United States.

Like any online service, these providers receive your device's IP address when the app connects to them, as part of handling the connection.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the security of the service.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and training data: until you delete it in the app or delete your account.
  • AI import files and results: as described in AI import.
  • Crash reports: up to 30 days, then deleted by Sentry.

7. Deleting your account

To delete your NoMoreX account and its data, email therealnerdybeast@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes:

  • your account, email address and profile;
  • your personal bests, workouts, programs, logged sessions and custom exercises;
  • any AI import files and results.

Copies other users saved of things you published belong to them and aren't removed (see What other people can see). Crash reports expire on their own schedule (see How long we keep it).

Without deleting your account, you can delete personal bests and workouts in the app at any time. Programs can be archived in the app; deleting your account removes them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, get a copy of it, or object to how it's used. To make a request, email therealnerdybeast@gmail.com. We'll respond within the time the law allows. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections. Each user's data is separated at the database level, so one account can't read another's private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

10. Children

NoMoreX isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is used, we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
