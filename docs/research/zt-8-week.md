# ZT 8 Week

**Source:** Google Drive → Weightlifting → `ZT 8 Week` (shared by mkpenrod05@gmail.com)
**Files transcribed (real, from source PDFs):**
- `8 Week Block Week 1 Training.pdf`
- `8 Week Block Week 2 Training.pdf`
- `8 Week Block Week 3 Training.pdf`

**⚠️ Weeks 4–8 do not exist in the shared Drive folder.** Only weeks 1–3 were found. Per instructions, weeks 4–8 below are **authored/estimated by Claude**, not transcribed from a source document — see the banner on each of those weeks and the design rationale at the bottom of this file. Treat every set in Weeks 4–8 as a placeholder for reference/prototyping only, not as the real program.

**Length:** 8 weeks, 6-day week (Day 4 is always low-impact cardio + yoga/stretching — a recovery day, not a lifting day).

## How to read the "Basis" tags

Percentage-based sets are tagged `[Basis: <Exercise> max]` for the lift the percentage is calculated from. `%ofCJ` appears explicitly in the source for several lines (percentage of Clean & Jerk max applied to a different exercise) — this is the clearest explicit basis-tagging example in the whole corpus and a good model for how the app might let a user tag a set's basis lift.

## Design rationale for estimated Weeks 4–8

Only 3 of 8 weeks existed in the source folder. Weeks 4–8 were authored to fit the pattern visible across weeks 1–3 of this program and the periodization style seen across the other three ZT programs in this reference set:

- The same 6-day structure is preserved throughout (Day 1: Snatch/Jerk complex + Front Squat; Day 2: Power Clean/Push Press/Strict Press; Day 3: technical classic-lift work + Front Squat + jumps; Day 4: recovery/cardio+yoga; Day 5: block work or accessory; Day 6: heavy classic-lift work + posterior chain/carries).
- Week 4 is a deload (lower % and volume), matching the deload placement seen at the week-4 mark of `ZT 6 Week Program` and the built-in "DELOAD NEXT WEEK!" note in `ZT Strong Like Bull`.
- Weeks 5–6 resume and exceed Week 3's intensity, consistent with the loading→intensification pattern in the other ZT programs.
- Week 7 further intensifies and drops volume, mirroring the "Peak Week" pattern in `ZT - 6 Week Positional`.
- Week 8 tapers to a test/meet day, mirroring both `ZT - 6 Week Positional` (Week 6, 1RM Snatch/Clean & Jerk on the final lifting day) and `ZT Strong Like Bull` (Week 4, days-out taper into "Compete").

**Do not treat Weeks 4–8 as real program data.** They exist only to give the app's data model something realistic-looking to prototype against for an 8-week block; if/when the real weeks 4–8 turn up, this file should be replaced with the actual transcription.

## Notes / ambiguities for app design

- `%ofCJ` in the source is the clearest explicit basis annotation in the whole corpus — a strong signal that the program builder should let a user attach an explicit "based on" exercise to any percentage set rather than always inferring it from the exercise name.
- Several sets (Day 2 lifts in particular) reference "this week's build-to-max" and then percentage that same session's result on a later day, rather than referencing a stored PB at all — a "reference this week's earlier result" concept, distinct from both absolute and PB-percentage set types.
